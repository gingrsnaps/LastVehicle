VALIDATION
validate_glb.py: structure, skin weights, clip names/durations, loop closure.
validate_preservation.py previous.glb: exact previous-channel comparison.
check_poses.py: sampled floor/reach checks using the evaluated skinned mesh.
make_contact_sheet.py: combines actual Blender renders for inspection.

The contact sheet was visually inspected. New planted Peek, DoorPush and
CrouchSearch feet drift less than 0.3 mm across the measured samples. Stalk
and StartRun feet intentionally move. These results do not establish browser
animation blending, runtime contact alignment, or subjective scare quality.
StartRun joins Run phase zero with matrix error zero. CrouchSearch fingertips
were lifted after a full-mesh floor audit; only submillimetre interpolated sole
penetration remains in the sampled new clips. Browser results need an actual
game playtest. No runtime IK/physics dependency is added.
